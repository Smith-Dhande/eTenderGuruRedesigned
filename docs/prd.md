# eTender Guru — Product Requirements Document (PRD)

**Document Type:** Product Requirements Document  
**Product:** eTender Guru  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Rebuild / New Development

---

## 1. Product Overview

eTender Guru is an educational platform focused on helping individuals and businesses understand government tenders and e-tendering.

The platform is centered around the expertise of the eTender Guru owner and provides users with structured learning through courses, free educational content through YouTube videos, information about the owner, and other tender-related resources.

The website should act as the central digital platform for the eTender Guru brand, bringing together:

- The owner's expertise and profile
- Educational courses
- YouTube videos
- Educational resources
- Testimonials and credibility information
- Contact and enquiry options

The primary objective is to help visitors understand what eTender Guru offers, learn from the available free content, explore courses, and get in touch for further guidance or enrollment.

---

# 2. Product Goals

The main goals of the eTender Guru platform are:

1. Establish eTender Guru as an educational platform for government tender and e-tendering knowledge.
2. Showcase the expertise and credibility of the owner.
3. Showcase available educational courses.
4. Showcase the owner's YouTube educational content.
5. Provide useful free educational resources.
6. Allow visitors to understand which courses are relevant to them.
7. Generate course enquiries and potential enrollments.
8. Provide visitors with easy ways to contact eTender Guru.
9. Build trust through owner information, testimonials, achievements, and educational content.
10. Create a centralized online presence for the eTender Guru brand.

---

# 3. Target Audience

The platform is intended for people who want to learn about government tenders and e-tendering.

Potential users include:

- Beginners interested in government tenders
- Contractors
- Business owners
- Entrepreneurs
- Suppliers
- Vendors
- Tendering professionals
- Existing tender participants
- Individuals looking to understand e-tendering
- People who want structured training related to government tenders

The exact audience can be refined based on the actual courses and services offered by eTender Guru.

# 15. Content Relationships

The platform should connect related content wherever appropriate.

For example:

**Course → Related YouTube Videos**

A course can have related free YouTube videos.

**Course → Related Resources**

A course can have related educational resources.

**YouTube Video → Related Course**

A video can direct interested viewers toward a relevant course.

**Owner → Courses**

The owner's profile should be associated with the courses they teach.

**Owner → YouTube**

The owner's profile should connect to their YouTube educational content.

This creates a connected educational ecosystem.

# 28. Final Product Definition

eTender Guru is a **personal-expert-led educational platform for government tender and e-tendering knowledge**.

The platform brings together the owner's expertise, structured courses, free YouTube education, educational resources, credibility information, and direct enquiry channels.

The core product ecosystem is:

**Owner Expertise**
→ establishes knowledge and credibility

**YouTube Content**
→ provides free education and attracts visitors

**Courses**
→ provides structured/deeper education

**Resources**
→ provides additional learning

**Enquiries**
→ converts interested visitors into potential students/customers

The website should therefore function as the central digital presence for the eTender Guru brand and provide a clear path from **discovering the expert → learning from free content → exploring courses → making an enquiry**.
