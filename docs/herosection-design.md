## eTender Guru — Hero Section Implementation

Rebuild the eTender Guru landing-page hero based on the approved visual reference.

IMPORTANT:
Do not redesign or reinterpret the structure. Follow the reference composition closely.

### 1. Hero Structure

Create a single hero/banner section that:

- Starts directly at the very top of the page.
- Has NO white space above it.
- Has NO visible upper-left or upper-right rounded corners.
- Touches the top edge of the viewport.
- Has white space/margins ONLY on the left and right sides.
- Occupies approximately 60% of the viewport height.
- Has rounded lower-left and lower-right corners.
- The white page/background continues naturally below the hero.
- Do not make the hero full-page height.

The result should feel like an orange visual banner sitting inside the page while still being directly connected to the top of the viewport.

### 2. Founder Image

Use the actual founder image from:

/public/foundernobg.png

DO NOT:
- Generate a replacement person.
- Use stock photography.
- Modify the person's identity.
- Replace the image with another image.

The image is already a transparent PNG and should be positioned as a cutout.

The founder should occupy the right side of the hero.

The person should feel integrated into the composition rather than placed inside a rectangular image container.

The founder can extend slightly beyond the lower boundary of the orange banner.

IMPORTANT:
The left hand should be the hand that visually extends outside the main layout/boundary as shown in the reference.

Do not allow the other hand to awkwardly break outside the composition.

### 3. Hero Background

Use the approved orange background from the reference.

Keep the background clean and largely uninterrupted.

Do not introduce:
- Gradients
- Glassmorphism
- Heavy textures
- Stock imagery
- Large decorative illustrations
- Excessive visual effects

Subtle graphical elements such as thin arcs, lines, small marks, or slide indicators are acceptable if they support the composition.

### 4. Typography

Keep the text minimal.

Primary heading:

"Know
Tenders."

Use the same editorial relationship shown in the reference:

- "Know" should have stronger visual weight.
- "Tenders." should feel lighter in comparison.
- Keep the heading large enough to establish hierarchy, but NOT excessively huge.
- It should remain comfortably contained within the left side of the banner.

Supporting text:

"Practical tender education from real-world experience."

Keep it short and readable.

### 5. CTA

Include one primary CTA:

"EXPLORE COURSES"

Use a simple arrow treatment.

Do not add multiple CTA buttons.

### 6. Small Supporting Label

Above the main heading, include:

"GOVERNMENT TENDERS MADE SIMPLE"

Keep it small with generous letter spacing.

### 7. Decorative Elements

Use decoration very sparingly.

Possible elements:

- Thin curved arc
- Small circular marker
- Handwritten-style annotation:
  "Knowledge
   Today
   Opportunities
   Tomorrow"
- Small arrow pointing toward the founder
- Minimal slide indicator such as:
  01
  02
  03

These elements should remain secondary to the founder and headline.

Do not overcrowd the hero.

### 8. Layout

The composition should roughly follow:

LEFT
- Small label
- Main heading
- Short description
- CTA

CENTER / TRANSITION
- Subtle decorative arc
- Small supporting annotation

RIGHT
- Founder cutout

The founder should visually overlap the orange banner boundary slightly at the bottom.

### 9. White Margins

This is important.

The orange banner should have:

LEFT → white margin
RIGHT → white margin
TOP → NO white margin
BOTTOM → white space after the rounded corners

Conceptually:

┌───────────────────────────────┐
│       ORANGE HERO             │
│                               │
│  Content              Founder │
│                               │
└╮                             ╭┘
  ───── white page below ──────

Do NOT create a white margin above the orange section.

### 10. Responsive Behavior

On desktop, preserve the reference composition.

On tablet/mobile:

- Keep the hero approximately 60% viewport height where practical.
- Reposition the founder instead of shrinking everything excessively.
- Prevent text from colliding with the founder.
- Maintain the orange banner and rounded lower corners.
- Preserve the visual hierarchy.
- Ensure the founder remains clearly visible.

Do not simply stack everything into generic mobile cards.

### 11. Overall Quality

The hero should feel:

- Modern
- Premium
- Editorial
- Minimal
- Confident
- Content-focused

Avoid generic AI-generated landing-page patterns.

Do not add:
- Navbar
- Extra sections inside the hero
- Statistics
- Testimonials
- Feature cards
- Multiple buttons
- Large amounts of marketing copy
- Generic decorative blobs
- Generic gradients

The hero should remain visually simple and rely on:
1. Orange background
2. Strong typography
3. Founder cutout
4. Minimal supporting copy
5. One CTA
6. Subtle graphic details
